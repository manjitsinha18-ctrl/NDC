Frequently Asked Questions (अक्सर सोधिने प्रश्नहरू)

How many years difference is there between BS and AD?

Bikram Sambat (वि.सं.) is approximately 56 years, 8 months and 17 days ahead of the Gregorian Anno Domini (ई.सं.) calendar. For example, 2024 AD corresponds broadly to 2080–2081 BS.

Why can’t simple addition or subtraction convert BS to AD?

Nepali calendar months vary in length between 29 to 32 days depending on solar transit through zodiac signs (Rashi). Each year's month lengths must be tracked using astronomical almanac records.

Does this converter work without an Internet connection?

Yes! All calendar lookup matrices from 2000 BS to 2090 BS are stored directly within this single index.html file, requiring no external API requests or server dependencies.

What are the Devanagari numerals used in Nepal?

Devanagari numerals are ० (0), १ (1), २ (2), ३ (3), ४ (4), ५ (5), ६ (6), ७ (7), ८ (8), and ९ (9). This utility converts dates seamlessly between Roman and Nepali Devanagari notation.
